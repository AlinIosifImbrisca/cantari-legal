2. About the song collection

The app contains the Romanian texts of 1,170 Christian hymns, numbered as in a hymnbook titled Cântările Evangheliei. Many are Romanian translations or adaptations of hymns first written in other languages. The data in the app does not name the authors, translators or composers of the individual songs.

The abbreviations shown with some songs (for example H. Ev., Glbst., C. Tr.) refer to other hymnbooks in which the same song appears. They are given only to help identify the songs and do not imply any connection with those publications.

The app is not an official publication of any church, union of churches or hymnbook publisher.

3. Rights status — stated openly

A hymn being old, sung in churches or printed in a hymnbook does not by itself mean that its text is free to reproduce. The original text, each translation or adaptation, the music and the edited collection can each be protected separately, generally until 70 years after the death of their author.

For this reason the rights in every song are being reviewed. At the date above, no song in the app has yet been verified as being in the public domain or as covered by a license. The developer is seeking the necessary permissions from the rights holders, and will correct, credit or remove content according to the outcome.

The procedure below lets rights holders contact the developer. It does not replace the permission the developer needs, and the developer does not treat it as such.

4. Rights holders: how to contact me

If you are an author, translator, composer, heir, publisher or other rights holder — or act on behalf of one — write to christiansongs822@gmail.com with the subject Rights request – song [number]. In the app you can also use Report a problem with this song at the end of each song, or Settings › Legal & privacy › Rights-holder request.

Please include:

  1. Your name and contact details and, if you act for someone else, on whose behalf.
  2. The song number(s) and title(s) in the app.
  3. The work or contribution concerned (text, translation, music, collection) and the basis of your rights (for example: author, heir, publisher, licensee), with any supporting evidence such as a publication reference, contract or declaration.
  4. What you are asking for: a credit or correction, removal, or a discussion about permission or a license.
  5. A statement that the information you provide is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

What happens next:

  • I confirm receipt within 7 days and tell you if anything else is needed.
  • I review the request, normally within 30 days. If the claim appears well founded, the affected content is withheld from the next version of the app until the matter is resolved, and I tell you what I have done.
  • If you grant permission or a license, the required credits and conditions are applied in the app.
  • Because the app works offline, changes reach users when they update the app; I cannot change copies already installed.

Your request and the related correspondence are handled as described in section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

5. Corrections

If you find a wrong word, a missing verse or another error in a song, please write to the same address with the song number. Corrections are made only from a reliable source, such as the printed hymnbook, and are recorded.
