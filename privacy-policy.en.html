Privacy Policy

Cântările Evangheliei — Android app (package ro.cantarileevangheliei)

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Version 1.1 · Applies to app version 1.3.0 and later

In short

  • The app works fully offline. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and it does not request the Android permission to access the internet.
  • Your favorites, reading history and settings are stored only on your device. The developer cannot see them.
  • If you turn on Android backup, Android can include these app settings in your device backup, as explained below.
  • If you choose to send a voluntary tip, you do so on PayPal's website; the app never handles your payment details.
  • The developer receives personal data only if you choose to write an email or to send a tip.

2. What the app stores on your device

To provide the features you use, the app keeps the following information in its private storage on your device:

  • Favorites — the numbers of the songs you mark with a heart.
  • Reading history — the numbers of the last 12 songs you opened.
  • Preferences — theme, interface text size and font, lyric size, line spacing, chorus repetition, keep-screen-on, reading-position and start-up options, song order and interface language.
  • Temporary state — the text of your current search, the song that is open and your reading position. This is kept in memory and in Android's saved-state mechanism so that it survives screen rotation, and it is discarded when the app's task is closed.

This information is not sent anywhere by the app, and other apps cannot read it. The developer has no access to it. It contains no name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

Because this information never leaves your device through the app, the developer does not collect or process it.

3. Android backup and transfer to a new phone

The app allows Android's built-in backup service to include favorites, reading history and preferences, so that they can be restored when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. Nothing else is included, and the song catalog is part of the app itself.

  • Backups are made by Android and Google, not by the app, and only if backup is turned on in your device settings. They are stored in your Google account and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. The developer cannot access them.
  • On Android 12 and later, the app allows cloud backup only when your device can encrypt the backup end-to-end (which requires a screen lock). On Android 9 to 11, Android encrypts backups with your screen lock when one is set; on older Android versions this encryption may not be available.
  • When you move to a new phone with a cable or a direct device-to-device transfer, the same information can be copied directly between your devices.
  • You can turn backup off at any time in your device settings (usually Settings › Google › Backup or Settings › System › Backup). Deleting the app does not by itself delete a backup that already exists in your Google account; you can manage or delete backups in your device or Google account settings.

4. Sharing and copying song texts

When you tap Share, the app gives the song text to the app you choose in Android's share menu (for example a messaging app). What happens next is governed by that app. When you tap Copy, the song text is placed on your device's clipboard. In neither case is anything sent to the developer.

Versions of the app in which a support page has been set up show a Support the Developer option. A tip is voluntary and unlocks nothing: every song and feature is free whether or not you contribute.

11. Deleting your information

  • Reading history: Settings › Personal data › Clear reading history.
  • Favorites: tap the heart again on a song, or remove it from the Favorites tab.
  • Preferences: Settings › Personal data › Reset settings (the interface language is kept; change it under Settings › Library › Language).
  • Everything: in Android, open Settings › Apps › Cântările Evangheliei › Storage and choose Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Backups already stored in your Google account are managed in your device or Google account settings (see section 3).
  • Emails you sent me: ask me to delete them at the address above.
